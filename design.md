# ALATPay Streams Design Guidelines

This document details the unified visual, layout, and copywriting guidelines for the ALATPay Streams platform to ensure design consistency.

## 1. Visual Theme & Color Palette
*   **Theme**: Clean, high-fidelity light theme with off-white backdrops (`#FAFAFB`) and flat white cards.
*   **Accent Color**: Premium **Royal Blue** (`#2563EB` / Tailwind `blue-600`) as the primary brand color for highlights, active links, and buttons.
*   **Hover States**: Deep Royal Blue (`#1D4ED8` / Tailwind `blue-700`).
*   **Subtle Highlights**: Light Blue (`#EFF6FF` / Tailwind `blue-50`) for pill backdrops or alert backgrounds.
*   **Success Indicators**: Emerald Green (`#0E9F6E`).

## 2. Typography
*   **Headings**: `Space Grotesk` (geometric, clean, tracking-[-0.03em] for display sizes).
*   **Body & UI Copy**: `Inter` (sans-serif, optimized for readability and alignment).
*   **Code & Math Logs**: `IBM Plex Mono` (monospace, clear character separation).

## 3. Layout Spacing & Alignment
*   **Left-Alignment**: Landing page hero copy (headline, body paragraph, and CTA button) must remain strictly left-aligned.
*   **Section Dividers**: No hard horizontal divider border lines (`border-t`) are allowed between sections. Use vertical padding or subtle shadows to group content.
*   **Full-Bleed Canvas**: Dashboards and analytics views must occupy the full content area. Do not constrain data columns with unnecessary chatbot elements or voice panels.

## 4. Copy & Symbols Constraints
*   **Punctuation**: No em-dashes (`—`) in headers or descriptive paragraphs. Use standard commas, periods, or colons.
*   **Symbols**: No text forward-arrow icons or chevrons (`→`, `&rarr;`, `ChevronRight`) in headers or buttons.
*   **Fintech Vocabulary**: Stick to professional fintech terms. Avoid informal names.
    *   *Correct*: "Sub-account" or "Ledger", "Tax Settlement", "Capital Reserve", "Accounts Payable", "Payroll".
    *   *Incorrect*: "Buckets", "VAT Reserve", "Emergency Savings".
