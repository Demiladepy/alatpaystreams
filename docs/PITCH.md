# ALATPay Streams — Pitch Deck (key slides)

## Slide 5 — How it works (the mechanic)

**Income lands → rules split → buckets fill → AI acts**

| Pillar | Consumer (ALAT) | Merchant (Streams) |
|--------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Voice** | SAW voice banking | **Streams Voice** — speak to configure buckets, check sales, accept loans |
| **Contactless** | Tap and Pay | **Tap & Pay routing** — NFC payments split like any other channel |
| **Predictive uptime** | Recipient bank availability | **Streams Uptime** — pre-disbursement rail health before loan & supplier payouts |

**Fourth column headline:**  
*Voice. Contactless. Predictive uptime. The merchant counterpart to ALAT: The Evolution.*
